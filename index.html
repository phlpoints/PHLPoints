Maximize your miles at PHL

Maximizing credit card points in Philadelphia is not like any other big city, because Philadelphia is dominated by American Airlines. Unlike the other major airlines, American is not a transfer partner with any of the most popular travel cards which means that you can not turn Amex Platinum or Chase Sapphire Reserve points into AAdvantage miles. Because Philadelphia does not have a strong presence from United, Delta, Southwest etc., not having access to AAdvantage miles means you do not have access to most flights out of PHL. Therefore, if you want the best flights and the best deals, you must commit to the AAdvantage program.

I have been searching for free or discounted flights out of PHL for over 20 years. It all started with a free Bank of America Travel Card and, over the last 20 years, I have analyzed every major travel credit card for use out of PHL. Throughout these next pages, I will explain how I have maximized the AAdvantage program and fly my family around the world out of PHL for free.

Overview

Understanding the difference between bonus miles and Loyalty Points is necessary to maximize your miles. It is slightly confusing, but the simple answer is that Loyalty Points earn you status with the airline, while bonus miles are what you use to book your award flights. You need both to get free flights and the highest level of service from American Airlines.

Bonus miles

Bonus miles accrue as bonuses provided by American Airlines that can be used for award flights but do not go toward your status with the airline. For example, the sign-up bonus on a credit card will give you bonus miles to spend on flights, but that extra does not go toward your status.

Loyalty Points

Loyalty Points are probably the most important of the two, because each Loyalty Point you earn also comes with a mile you can spend on a future flight, but it also earns you status with the airline. As further outlined in the Status section, status provides you with perks that improve your experience. The most valuable perk provided by status is free seat upgrades within the Main Cabin.

AA Earning System

Unlike the other major credit cards who offer multipliers by specific category (for example, 3x points on all restaurant purchases), the AA system offers smaller multipliers by category but provides large multipliers by store. Their system works by pushing users to the AA specific websites where you can earn large multipliers by specific store. Continue on to the next three sections on AA Hotels, AA Shopping, and AA Dining to learn more.

The AA Hotels portal

The AA Hotels portal is where you will maximize your points. This is where I turn a single hotel stay into a round-trip flight. If you follow the steps below, you can earn both Loyalty Points and bonus miles that will let you fly for free, with the perks that come with American's highest status levels.

If you follow these steps, you'll earn an outrageously high number of bonus miles and Loyalty Points. Below are a few examples of how I've used the AA Hotels portal to get free flights for years.

Book on the AA Hotels portal

Search for and choose your hotels on the AAdvantage Hotels website. This portal does not offer the cheapest rooms and is usually slightly more expensive than other sites, but you must use this site to receive the maximum return.

Pay with your AA Executive card

Paying with the Citi AAdvantage Executive card earns 12x miles per dollar. The Globe card earns 6x. The Platinum Select card earns 3x starting Jan 24, 2027.

Maximize your Loyalty Point award

This is what makes the AA Hotels portal worth it. On top of the 12x bonus miles, you're awarded Loyalty Points once your hotel stay is complete. If you log in and pay with an AA credit card, the award is usually 15,000 Loyalty Points, and you can often pay a slight upcharge for a larger award.

The AA Shopping portal

AA Shopping is American's online shopping portal. Click through it before you buy from a store you already use, and the store pays you miles and Loyalty Points on top of whatever your credit card earns.

If you follow the steps below, everyday online purchases turn into a steady stream of miles and Loyalty Points.

Log in to the AA Shopping portal

Go to the AAdvantage eShopping website and sign in with your AAdvantage number. More than 1,200 online stores take part, from department stores to Apple and travel sites. The free Chrome browser button can remind you when a site you're on pays miles.

Click through to the store

Find the store and check its rate, typically 1 to 10 miles per dollar. Clicking through sends you to the store's own site with a tracking link. Start every order from the portal, and avoid coupon codes the store doesn't list, or the order may not track.

Pay with your AA credit card

Check out as usual in the same browser session. Paying with an AAdvantage card adds the card's miles and 1 Loyalty Point per dollar on top of the portal miles. If SimplyMiles has a matching offer, activate it first and the same purchase pays a third time.

Watch for the confirmation emails

The portal emails you when it tracks the order. Miles usually post within a few days but can take up to 15 business days. If they're still missing after that, file a claim with eShopping customer service.

AA Dining

AA Dining pays you miles and Loyalty Points for eating out. Register a card once, then eat at participating restaurants as usual, and the miles post automatically.

If you follow the steps below, the meals you're already eating in Philly earn you up to 5 miles and 5 Loyalty Points per dollar.

Join and link your cards

Sign up on the AAdvantage Dining website with your AAdvantage number and register the credit and debit cards you use to eat out. Any card works, not just AA cards. A meal only tracks if you pay with a registered card, so add your partner's cards too and re-register any card that gets replaced.

Opt in to emails

This one setting triples what you earn, from 1 to 3 miles per dollar. There's no reason to skip it.

Eat at participating restaurants

Search the site or app for participating places near you, from Center City to the suburbs. Pay with a registered card and the visit tracks on its own, with nothing to show the server. Paying with the Platinum Select or Globe card adds another 2 miles per dollar at restaurants.

Reach VIP

After 11 qualifying visits in a calendar year, you become VIP and earn 5 miles per dollar starting with your 12th. VIP counts visits, not dollars, so a few cheap lunches or coffees early in the year unlock 5x for every bigger dinner after. VIP lasts through the next full calendar year.

Redeem · Stop 05 of 11

American Airlines Miles

If you have read this far and followed the program, you have amassed tens or hundreds of thousands of AA miles. But if you do not use your points wisely, you will waste them on expensive award flights that undermine all of the work you put in. To avoid that fate, follow these tips.

Book early

It may sound crazy, but the best deals for flights to desirable destinations occur 11 months in advance. Every example I provided in the AA Hotels section was booked the first day the flights were available. While this may not be practical for some, award flights are usually fully refundable, so you have the freedom to lock in the flight at a great rate with the ability to cancel later on.

Flexible dates

The best fares are not always on the exact dates you need. When searching for award fares, the American app gives you the option to check flights on dates surrounding your preferred flight. Many times, you will see a Thursday flight being thousands of points less than a Friday flight.

Flexible routes

The best fares are available in cities you may not expect. If you try to force an exact destination, you may find the award fare is expensive. To avoid that, be willing to fly into or out of another city, as the difference in price could be 100,000 or more miles. For example, when looking for an award flight for my family next year, we wanted to visit Northern Italy, but flights into Venice were 250,000 miles for one round-trip fare while flights into Milan were 35,000 miles for one round-trip fare. While it's a different trip, you can get within a few hours' train ride of Venice for over 200,000 miles less just by checking a similar city.

Put it into practice

British Airways Avios

If you are insistent that you do not want to access the AAdvantage system or any of the associated credit cards, there is a complicated end around with the other major credit cards that can allow you to transfer miles to British Airways which is partnered with American Airlines. This can work and I used this system for years but it is not as strong as the AAdvantage system for a few reasons.

Limited Flights

Not every American Airlines flight is offered as a reward flight by British Airways. If the cost of the flight is more than the British Airways system allows, they will not even offer you the flight.

High International Fees and Taxes

British Airways charges large fees for international flights from the US to most places in Europe. So even if you do find an available British Airways flight from PHL, it will likely come with a large fee that will make the cost of the flight more considering the amount of miles required. I have seen reward flights to Europe with over $1,000 in fees when the cost of the flight is around that price. That being said, this system does not charge these large fees for domestic flights and flights to London.

No Status

Transferring points from other major credit cards does not provide any status with the airline. In short, this system will get you on the plane but you will be boarding late and be in the back of the plane. Granted, if you chose a credit card with a great airport lounge, it offsets some of that. But I prefer the combination of free flights and the perks of status with the airline.

Conclusion

This system can work in limited areas but you will miss out on many flights and will certainly not be able to access highly desired timeframes and destinations where no flights are awarded. I use this system to target first class fares to London. If you do not want to switch to the AAdvantage program, below is how to access flights out of PHL.

How to book American flights with Avios

Use a Card that Transfers Points to British Airways

In order to utilize this system, you need to utilize a credit card that is a transfer partner with British Airways. For example, Amex Membership Rewards, Chase Ultimate Rewards, Capital One, and Wells Fargo points all transfer to Avios one-to-one. You can use their earning systems (which are simple and plentiful) to amass a large amount of points as outlined by those companies.

Open a Free British Airways Executive Club Account

You need to open a British Airways Executive Club Account and have access to your membership number. This is the account that the credit card points will be transferred to. It is typically fast and easy to transfer points to your Executive Club Account from your credit card account but, be warned, the BA.com website is weirdly glitchy at times.

Search For Avios Flights

On BA.com, you need to search for flights under a separate search dedicated to an Avios Flight. Avios are what BA calls their miles. As noted above, you will be able to access American Airlines flights out of PHL on this system if they are low enough in price but there will be limitations. Check the miles and the taxes and fees before you book anything to make sure it is not a trap fare that is actually more expensive than buying the fare with money.

Check the Cost for First Class Fares

One of the best perks of British Airways system is that it can be the cheapest award fare for First Class fares. Recently, I booked four First Class tickets for my family to London for around 320,000 Avios total while coach was 100,000 Avios. This was a screaming deal, as they say, so I recommend checking First Class if you follow this system to see the price difference. Please be mindful that the First Class seats are targeted early and they are limited, so you will need to book those 12 months out (British releases their flights a month earlier for some reason) in order to access them.

Philly Lounges

American runs four lounges at PHL: a Flagship Lounge for international premium travelers and three Admirals Clubs spread across the airport. Here's where each one is, who can get in, and what to expect for food and amenities.

The A-West lounges are the newest and best, and the food and drinks at the Flagship Lounge are fantastic. The Admirals Clubs at the B/C and F gates are more than serviceable, offer free drinks and food, and save you a long walk on domestic and regional flights.
