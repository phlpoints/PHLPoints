Maximize your miles at PHL

I am a husband and father of two who has lived in Philadelphia for over 20 years. Throughout that time, I have utilized almost every major Credit Card Mileage Program to obtain free or discounted flights. It all started with a free Bank of America Travel Card that I used to get statement credits for airline purchases. Then I utilized the Chase Sapphire Reserve, Capital One Venture X, and the American Express Platinum cards. All of these cards were great for accruing points (and two of them have or had good lounges in PHL) but, because none are transfer partners with American Airlines, none could provide the best deals on the best flights out of Philadelphia and none provided status with any airline.

Finally, I found the American Airlines Credit Cards through Citi and discovered that not only could I get free flights but I could also obtain the highest levels of American Airlines status without spending any additional money. I have used these cards to fly my family of four around the world multiple times a year with the highest level of status without spending a single extra dollar. I am able to do this not because I am spending more money but because I make strategic choices to maximize American Airlines miles on every dollar I spend. Moreover, because American Airlines allows you to simultaneously earn status while earning miles, I have accrued hundreds of thousands of miles while achieving Executive Platinum Status with American Airlines. Through this program, we have flown in luxury with routine upgrades, preferred seats, almost unlimited baggage on flights, and the highest levels of service. Through this website, I will teach you how I utilize the American Airlines Credit Cards and their Loyalty Program. Along the way, I will also update the best Philadelphia flights, shopping, dining, and hotel deals I can find. I hope you all find this website helpful on your journeys.
